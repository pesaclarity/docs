# Plan: the double-entry journal (decision 0018)

Each stage ships on its own, with `tool/check` passing and the replay test growing.

1. **Journal underneath, nothing visible changes.** *Shipped in app #222 (schema 34). The journal was kept up to date, but nothing read it yet: balances, Home and every figure still came from the transaction rows until stage 2.* Tables `journal_entries` (id, occurred_at, source document, memo, reverses) and `postings` (entry, account, amount_minor signed, currency, rate). System accounts: Not yet sorted, Money in transit, Fuliza owed (one per M-Pesa account), Opening balances, Balance corrections, Fuliza charges, plus one per category. Entries are append-only: a changed or removed source posts a reversal and a new entry. A posting engine turns each existing transaction row (and its role, fee, Fuliza split, transfer pair) into an entry; a migration rebuilds every space's journal, idempotently, and logs accounts that don't reconcile. Invariant test: every entry sums to zero per currency.
2. **Balances from postings, reported balances as checks.** *Shipped in app #226 (schema 35). Balances, On hand, safe to spend and every figure of money held read the journal; Insights and the Health Index count Fuliza charges from it (#230). A gap the records do not reach stays in Not yet sorted and moves to Balance corrections only when the person adds a correction.* Account balances, On hand and safe to spend read the journal. Each reported balance becomes an assertion with a pass/fail and a gap. Typed or suggested balances are claims until they reconcile or are confirmed. Unchecked messages post to Not yet sorted; Home shows how much is not yet sorted.
3. **Fixes as entries.** Set the balance, cash counts, corrections, unmatching and moving post reversing plus new entries; nothing is edited. Money in transit clears when transfers match, with fees.
4. **Everything else reads the journal.** Plan, Insights, Activity, reports, tax and the business Books become views over postings; the old "balance difference" plug goes.
5. **The replay test.** A synthetic month with unparsed messages, Fuliza draws and repayments, KCB → M-Pesa transfers with fees, a duplicate statement import and a wrongly typed balance; every account must match its reported balances, and On hand must match the sum the person would get by hand.

Out of scope here: moving money between spaces (still never), multi-currency own transfers (currencies stage 2).
